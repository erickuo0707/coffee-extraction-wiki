# S01 — Coffee extraction review

- **Citation:** Córdoba et al. (2020), “Coffee extraction: A review of parameters and their influence on the physicochemical characteristics and flavour of coffee brews,” *Trends in Food Science & Technology*, 96, 45–60.
- **DOI / publisher record:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tifs.2019.12.004
- **Source type:** Peer-reviewed review article.
- **Access checked:** 2026-11-15. Publisher search record and indexed abstract were available; the publisher full-text page could not be fetched in this session. This file preserves citation metadata and a paraphrase, not the article text.
- **Scope:** Reviews coffee extraction parameters, the physical and chemical processes involved, and relationships to beverage properties and flavor.
- **Ingested takeaways (paraphrased):** Extraction conditions interact. Grind distribution can change espresso-bed pressure and flow. Temperature, pressure, particle size, and water quality are among the factors associated with differences in chemical composition and flavor. Different aromatic compounds do not enter the beverage at identical rates.
- **Limits:** A review synthesizes earlier work rather than reporting one new controlled brew experiment. Results from different brewing methods and study designs should not be collapsed into one universal recipe.
- **Use in this Wiki:** Establishes the overview and motivates preserving the method and experimental conditions beside each claim. Do not use it alone to assert a precise optimal setting.
